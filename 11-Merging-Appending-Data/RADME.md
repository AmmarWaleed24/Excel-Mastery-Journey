# 🔗 Lesson 11: Combining Data (Append & Merge)

## 🎯 Objectives
Learning how to combine multiple tables or files into a single unified data source.

---

## 🛠 Skills Applied
- **Append Queries:** Stacking tables on top of each other (Merging January, February, and March data).
- **Merge Queries:** Performing "Lookups" inside Power Query (similar to XLOOKUP but more powerful) to join tables side-by-side.
- **Managing Relationships:** Connecting a "Sales Table" with a "Product Catalog" based on a Product ID.

## 📊 Practical Application
In the file `11-PowerQuery-Combine.xlsx`, I:
1. Appended three different monthly sales sheets into one master table.
2. Merged the master table with a "Price List" table to calculate total revenue automatically.
